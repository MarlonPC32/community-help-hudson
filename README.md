# LatiHudson – Community Help Map for Hudson County

**LatiHudson** is a bilingual digital resource designed to help residents of Hudson County, NJ — especially immigrants and underserved communities — locate **free food assistance, shelters, legal aid, and healthcare services**.

Built with **Google Sites** and **Google My Maps**, it presents the information in **English and Spanish**:

- An interactive map
- Categorized listings
- A button-based chatbot prototype for common questions (rule-based, not generative AI)

## Tools Used

- Google Sites
- Google My Maps
- Google Forms (feedback collection)
- Multilingual content (English / Spanish)
- Button-based chatbot prototype
- Canva (visual assets)

---

## Purpose

To support immigrant and low-income residents of New Jersey by centralizing essential service information in an accessible, bilingual digital format.

---

## Features

- Filterable interactive map with categorized service locations
- English and Spanish content for all key pages
- Chatbot prototype (button-based logic) to guide users through frequently asked questions
- Contact form for feedback and updates

---

## Background

- Built as a personal initiative based on real community needs
- Shared with immigrant peers and local families for feedback
- Distributed through word-of-mouth and community groups in Hudson County
